## What the agreed rules changed, and why

The source draft predates the rules being settled — it was written before the
snapshot existed, before per-account qualification was chosen, and before the
faucet-vs-buy problem was found. So this is a changelog against the rules, not
a list of mistakes in it. Four differences; three are the gap between a
promotion people trust afterward and one they argue about.

**1. "THE SNAPSHOT HAS ALREADY BEEN TAKEN" — now true, but the follow-on line
was not.** The draft said it was taken "immediately AFTER our Burn Wallet
finished locking its tokens." The lock was 2026-08-16; the snapshot is
2026-08-18T02:31Z. Two days is not "immediately after," and it is the one
sentence in the post that the whole fairness argument rests on. Replaced with
the actual slot numbers, which are stronger anyway — a slot is checkable, an
adverb isn't.

**2. "BUY & HODL" → "INCREASE your position."** A snapshot diff cannot tell a
purchase from a faucet drip or a gift from a friend. Saying "buy" would have
described something we do not measure. The 1,500 floor is the platform's
existing minimum-payout unit, not a number invented for this.

**3. Per ACCOUNT, not per wallet — and it needs the verification line.** Without
it, one person with twenty wallets takes twenty entries and everyone else's odds
quietly collapse. The requirement has to be visible BEFORE people participate;
discovering it at the close means someone did everything asked and still lost.
That single line is the most important addition to the post.

**4. "Still hold at the close" was missing entirely.** The rule exists either
way, so leaving it out means the first person to learn it is someone who sold
and lost an entry.

Also added: the end date (Aug 17 → Oct 1 — the draft had no end at all, and a
campaign with no end cannot be audited), the "adding liquidity counts as both"
rule stated openly rather than applied as a silent correction, the draw method
promised as publishable in advance, and the farm marked "proposed, not
committed" so nobody reads ~3.86M as a commitment.

Kept exactly as written: the voice, the jokes, the shovel line, Proof of Value /
Not Proof of Wealth, the disclaimer, the links, and the discretionary 6M tier
with its hedge intact.
