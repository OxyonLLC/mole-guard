## ⚠️ Before you pull

`git pull` on this machine can destroy runtime-written faucet data —
`src/faucet/airdrops.json` and `registrations.json` are written by `!register`
at runtime **and** tracked in git.

**This particular range does not touch those files** (verified: the only
`src/faucet/` change in `3a6729a..aaae1fb` is `bot.js`), so a plain `git pull`
is safe here. The rule still stands for future pulls, and `git reset --hard`,
`git checkout .` or `git stash` discard runtime writes regardless of what
changed upstream. Snapshot first if you use any of those.

**`verify/api-proxy.php` deserves its own step.** It gates *every* API call on
the site, so upload it deliberately and load one page afterwards to confirm the
site still answers, rather than sweeping it up in a bulk copy. It carries two
changes:

- it no longer echoes `curl_error()` to the browser, which was naming the
  backend tunnel host whenever the tunnel was down (register #26)
- it now forwards the `X-WinLEW-Session` header, which is what lets session
  tokens stop travelling in URLs (register #28)

**Upload order does not matter.** `hubApiFetch` detects a dropped header and
retries once with `?session=`, so a partial upload degrades to the old behaviour
instead of locking anyone out. Upload everything anyway — the degraded path is
exactly the tokens-in-URLs problem being removed.

### How to check it worked

1. Open `https://winlew.co/verify/profile.html` and confirm the dashboard
   populates (balances, milestones, rewards).
2. Look at the address bar and at the host's access log for that page: there
   should be **no `session=`** anywhere. If there is, the fallback is doing its
   job and `api-proxy.php` did not take — re-upload it.
3. Open `https://winlew.co/roadmap/` and confirm it says
   **"21 of 30 Platform Modules Live"**.

**This one needs the NODE BACKEND restarted, not just an upload.** The
calculation runs server-side in `/api/transparency/status`; the new page markup
is inert without it. Files: `src/mole/circulatingSupply.js` (new),

`src/public/index.html` in the static upload.

### What to check afterwards

1. `/transparency/` shows a **Circulating Supply** section under the mint card,
   reading **811,724,836.426356** — or a nearby figure, since it is live and the
   faucet reserve moves. It must never show 950,000,000 as circulating.
2. The storefront's "Circulating Supply" tile agrees with it. That tile used to
   show total supply under a circulating label; it now shows the real figure, and
   if the calculation is unavailable it relabels itself "Total Supply" instead of
   showing the wrong thing under the right name.
3. If the section says **"Circulating supply cannot be calculated right now"**,
   that is working as designed — one of the six escrow reads failed. Refresh. It
   deliberately shows nothing rather than a figure inflated by a missing lock.

### Two things that look wrong and are not

- **The burn wallet's 50,000,000 is counted as circulating.** Staged for burn is
  not burned; the tokens still exist and the mint still counts them. Deducting
  them would be the artificial-scarcity move this page exists to disprove.
- **All three reserve wallets are counted as circulating.** They are unlocked. In
  total 79,850,899 of project-controlled supply is left inside the figure —
  excluding it would report 8.4% less circulating supply, and that was declined
  deliberately.

Do not "correct" either of these. Full reasoning: `CIRCULATING-SUPPLY-AUDIT.md`.
