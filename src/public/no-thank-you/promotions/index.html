We don't pay for KOLs, influencers, callers, promo packages, "marketing partnerships," or any other pay-me-to-shill arrangement.

Not begging for someone else's.

What we will never do

  • Pay a KOL, influencer, caller, or "alpha group" to talk about $WinLEW.
  • Buy promotion packages, trending spots, engagement bots, or paid raids.
  • Pay for a "guaranteed listing," "guaranteed pump," or a paid price call.
  • Send tokens up front to anyone who DMs offering exposure.
  • Sponsor anyone to post a price prediction. Ever.
So if someone says WinLEW is paying them to promote it — they're lying. Not a misunderstanding, not a special case, not a deal you haven't heard about yet. There are none.

Why not?

Paid promotion buys attention from people who'd say the same words about any token that paid them, and it comes out of the same reserves that fund the faucet, the quiz, and the games. Every token spent renting someone's audience is a token not paid to the people actually showing up. Spending the community's supply to have strangers pretend to be the community is exactly backwards.

It's also the fastest way to end up with holders who were sold a price instead of a project. We'd rather grow slower and have the people here actually want to be here.

What we do instead — earn it, don't get gifted it

Everything is open on the same terms for everyone — no DM required, no follower count checked, no deal to negotiate:

  • Faucet — claim WinLEW on a cooldown, no permission and nobody's approval needed. Needs a linked wallet that already holds some WinLEW.
  • Quiz — learn the ecosystem and earn points free; redeeming those points for real WinLEW needs a linked wallet holding WinLEW.
  • The Mole Hub — play the games, earn from your own runs.
  • Bug bounties — find something real and broken, get paid for it.
  • Contribute — back the reserves and earn Clout, publicly ranked and on-chain verified.
  • Governance — one wallet, one vote. Verify your wallet and you get a vote, because you took the time. Buying extra weight on top of it is separate and needs 1,000,000 WinLEW held — but nobody has to buy anything to be heard.

That's the deal: contribution is the only thing that earns standing here. Nobody gets a bag for having an audience.

Got a "promo offer" DM?

Ignore it, and don't send anything. WinLEW will never DM you first asking for tokens, a wallet connection, or a seed phrase, and no one speaks for WinLEW in your DMs. If someone is impersonating the project or claiming a paid partnership, tell us in the community channels so other people don't fall for it.

Real, genuine collaborations happen in the open and get published — see Sponsors & Partners and the Transparency Center, where every reserve wallet is listed and independently verifiable on-chain. If it isn't on those pages, it isn't a WinLEW arrangement.
