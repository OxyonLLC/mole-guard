Verifying it's really us

Impersonation is the most common attack on a project like this, so the boundaries are worth stating plainly rather than leaving to judgement.

WinLEW will never:
  • DM you first, on Discord or anywhere else.
  • Ask for your seed phrase or private key. Not for support, not for "verification", not ever.
  • Ask you to move to a different site or app to "unlock", "validate" or "sync" your wallet.
  • Request payment for support, listings, roles, or a place in the community.

The only email addresses WinLEW uses are the three on this page, all ending in @winlew.co. Anything from a lookalike domain is not us. Genuine collaborations are published on Sponsors & Partners — if an arrangement isn't listed there, it doesn't exist. We also don't pay for promotion, so anyone claiming to be paid to shill $WinLEW is lying.
