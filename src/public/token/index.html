Supply numbers answer different questions

Total supply is the mint-level outstanding supply. Circulating supply uses the published exclusion method for specified locks and reserves. Locked balances are individual account balances, not a synonym for either supply number. Current values and calculation timestamps are shown in the Transparency Center; they are never substituted for one another.
