Every WinLEW Burn, In Order.

50,000,000 WinLEW no longer exists — half the long-term target. Here is every burn, and why our own ledger says a smaller number.

Supply started at one billion. It is now 950 million. Here is where the missing 50,000,000 went — starting with the three transactions anyone can open.

The real number is 50,000,000 — and half of it is the target

$WinLEW launched with 1,000,000,000 tokens. The mint reported 950,000,000 when this article was written. That difference is not an estimate or a running tally — it is the supply itself, and anyone can check it in one call:

50,000,000 WinLEW no longer exists.

That is 50% of the long-term 100M burn target, reached.

Updated August 19, 2026. Supply is no longer a round number: it now reads 949,999,960.615828. Two holders burned 39.384172 between them on August 18 — dust left in wallets they were closing. Nothing else moved, and the arithmetic below has been updated to match. This is what it looks like when a live number is published honestly instead of frozen at the moment it flattered us.

Why our own ledger says 49.7M

WinLEW's Burn Center confirms 49,707,077.187484 across 32 events. Against the on-chain total, that left 292,922.812516 unaccounted for in our records.

The gap is not missing tokens. It is burns we did not perform — holders who destroyed their own $WinLEW, some of it before the project began keeping this ledger at all. At least one was remembered: tokens sent out through the X faucet that the recipient burned the following day, entirely of their own accord.

That memory now has a transaction behind it. On August 19, 2026 we walked every wallet our own giveaway ledger names — 21 addresses the X Faucet Reserve has ever paid — and checked each one for burn instructions. Six turned up, totalling 100,830.472170 $WinLEW. The largest is the one that was remembered: a wallet received exactly 100,000 on January 1, 2025 and destroyed exactly 100,000 on January 3, signature here.

So the unexplained residual is now 192,131.724518, not 292,922.812516. It shrank because evidence arrived, not because we revised a guess.

Two things we will not dress up. These were account sweeps, not tributes — each one burns a leftover balance and closes the account to reclaim the rent, and the 100,000 was destroyed alongside two unrelated tokens in the same transaction. And the list is not complete and never can be: it covers wallets we paid, so anyone who bought on the open market and burned is still invisible to us. 100,830.472170 is a floor.

We could close the gap by simply publishing the 50M and saying nothing further. We publish both because they answer different questions. 50,000,000 is what the chain proves was destroyed. 49,707,077.187484 is what WinLEW can produce a transaction for. The second number is smaller precisely because it is the stricter claim, and the difference is other people choosing to burn tokens we never asked them to.

The three we executed

Of the destruction WinLEW carried out directly, three transactions used Solana's token burn instruction. They are listed above, and they total 26,785,536.931164 — the largest deliberate acts, not the whole story.

Moved is not burned

This is the distinction that matters most, and the one most easily blurred. Tokens sitting in the burn wallet are staged — still part of total supply, and still, in principle, moveable. Only the burn instruction actually reduces the mint's supply.

WinLEW's burn ledger records both, separately, and the progress bar counts only the second. That is why the "awaiting burn" figure and the "destroyed" figure are different numbers and always will be.

What changed in 2026

On August 16, 2026, 40,000,000 WinLEW was moved out of the burn wallet into two locks, leaving exactly 10,000,000 staged. The reason was tamper-proofing: burn-staged tokens used to sit somewhere the project could still spend from, and now they cannot be touched before their unlock dates.

Locked is still not burned. All 40M remains part of total supply and none of it counts toward burn progress.

The plan has changed, and the record shows it

Burn amounts have not followed a fixed schedule. Each has been larger than the last, and the long-term 100M target remains a goal rather than tokens already set aside anywhere. Where an earlier announcement said one thing and the outcome was another, the Burn Center keeps both — the announcement and what actually happened.

The next decision is due at the annual mark. Nothing has been committed beyond the 10,000,000 currently staged.
