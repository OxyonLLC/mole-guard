Two Years. December 7.

$WinLEW was created on December 7, 2024. On December 7, 2026, every registered wallet receives 10,000 $WinLEW.

Update — November 14, 2026: the mechanics

  • Amount: 10,000 $WinLEW per registration. It does not depend on how much you hold, and holding is not required.
  • How to register: in the WinLEW Discord, run !register <your Solana wallet or .sol name>.
  • Already registered? The 156 wallets registered as of November 14, 2026 are already in, and each counts once. You don't need to do anything.
  • New registrations count once per Discord account: the wallet your account has saved when registration closes. Registering more wallets adds nothing.
  • Registration closes: December 6, 2026 at 00:00 UTC. The list is fixed at that moment.
  • Sent: December 7, 2026, from the WinLEW treasury (diglew.sol).

Project-owned addresses and wallets banned from the faucet are left out.

$WinLEW was created on Solana on December 7, 2024. Its pump.fun page still says so, and anyone can check it. On December 7, 2026 — two years to the day — every registered wallet receives 10,000 $WinLEW.

How you can check it

The same standard as every other WinLEW claim: don't take our word for it.

  • The list can't grow quietly. The 156 wallets registered as of November 14 are frozen. Sorted, one per line, their SHA-256 is dfb9a484e71ba1dca699f3ebb3e4197b9d059e69c42d8e297a9bcbfea0a13733. The full list will be published with the snapshot, so anyone can recompute it.
  • The snapshot is public before anything is sent. When registration closes, the final list is committed to the XL1 blockchain and shown on the Oxyon Evidence Explorer before any token moves.
  • Every payout is on Solscan. After December 7, each payout's Solana transaction is listed and checked against the snapshot, wallet by wallet. Open any of them on Solscan yourself.

The original announcement (August 16, 2026)

Kept as published. When it was written, the mechanics had not been decided.

What is being announced

The date. That is genuinely all, and saying so now is the point: the date goes on the public record months ahead of the event rather than appearing afterwards.

What is not being announced

No amount. No snapshot. No eligibility rules. None of those have been decided, so none of them are being hinted at.

This matters more than it might seem. An airdrop post that implies a size, or suggests holding a particular amount will qualify you, is a promise of tokens — and once made, people act on it. Nothing here should be read as one. When the mechanics are set, they will be published here and recorded on Community Events, with the same detail every other WinLEW event carries.

Why the date is checkable

WinLEW's whole position is that claims should be verifiable, and "it's our anniversary" is a claim like any other. The creation date comes from the token's own pump.fun page, and it matches what Who We Are has said all along — that the token has been live on Solana mainnet since December 2024.

Two years of building. See you on the 7th.
