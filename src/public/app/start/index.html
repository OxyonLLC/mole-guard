Welcome to WinLEW

WinLEW is a community you take part in: browser games, a daily quiz, and leaderboards, with a Discord full of real people behind it.

  • Play the Mole Hub games and the XL1 Arcade
  • Answer the quiz and climb the leaderboards
  • Connect a wallet once, and come back any time

Two tokens, two networks

$WinLEW · Solana

$WinLEW is WinLEW's own token, on Solana. The daily drip is for wallets that already hold at least 1,000 $WinLEW.

$XL1 · XYO Layer One

$XL1 is the coin of XYO Layer One, a different network. XL1 rewards are distributed by Oxyon to a wallet you have verified.

One wallet for both: Oxyon Wallet. It holds $WinLEW and $XL1. Create a new wallet in it for WinLEW: you don't need to import a key from another wallet, and WinLEW never sees your keys. They stay on your phone.
