    Who does what.

    WinLEW gives people a reason to take part.Oxyon builds the systems that make it measurable and verifiable.XL1, by XYO Network, is the blockchain underneath.
    What is XL1?

    XYO Layer One, XYO Network's blockchain. It records blocks, transactions and proofs so results can be inspected independently. WinLEW and Oxyon build on it; neither runs or owns the network.

    xyo.network ↗

    Who is Oxyon?

    Oxyon is the infrastructure and evidence side of this ecosystem: it runs block producers on XL1, observes the chain, and builds GeoHackers and the tools behind wallet verification. Its dashboards are public.

    How Oxyon and WinLEW fit together ↗

    Run a producer

    Oxyon publishes the bundles it uses: a Windows (Docker) build and a Raspberry Pi build, with the scripts to run and watch them.
