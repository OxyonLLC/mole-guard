  2. 2Sign in and verify

    Sign in with Discord (accounts at least 30 days old earn), then prove the wallet is yours: tap Verify XL1 wallet above, or run /verify with the GeoHackers bot in our Discord. You sign a one-time message that costs and sends nothing.

    A connected wallet isn't verified yet: you're set when the box above says Ready to earn.

    On Android, if Discord opens the verify link in its own browser, tap Open in Oxyon Wallet there. We will never ask for your recovery phrase or private key.

  3. 3Play and earn

    Hold to multiply: cash out any reward after 24 h, or wait and it's multiplied when you cash out: 24 h ×1 · 72 h ×2 · 7 days ×5 · 30 days ×7.

How rewards work

The rules, in plain words.

XL1 for every minute of a finished round, held 24 h, then multiplied the longer you hold.Show every rule
  1. Sign in and verifySign in with Discord, then prove your XL1 wallet is yours. A signature only: nothing is sent.
  2. PlayEach minute of a finished round earns XL1, up to the round and daily limits.
  3. HoldRewards wait 24 h before you can cash out. Holding longer multiplies them.
  4. Cash outPaid on-chain from the arcade's distributor wallet. Every payment can be checked.
  • Paid for time, not score. Each minute of a finished round earns XL1, in every game except Grub Drop.
  • Limits. At most — minutes count per round, and — XL1 a day across all games, per account and per wallet.
  • Real play only. Time is paid only as far as the round's score backs it (each game has its own minimum score per second), a round with no score earns nothing, and you can play one round at a time.
  • A verified wallet. Rewards go only to an XL1 wallet you have proven is yours, linked to your Discord account, and the Discord account must be old enough to earn.
  • Hold to multiply. Rewards can be cashed out 24 h after you earn them, multiplied by how long they were held: —. Each reward's multiplier is set by its age when you cash out, and the daily maximum counts the amount before the multiplier.
  • Some cash-outs are checked. A moderator looks at unusually large or unusual cash-outs before they are paid. Everything else is paid automatically.
  • No promised value. XL1 rewards are real tokens on the XL1 network, with no promised monetary value.
How a round is checked

The arcade's server starts a session when a round begins and times it itself. When the round ends, the game reports its score, and the server pays for the time it measured, up to the round limit, and only as far as the score backs it up (each game has its own minimum score per second).

Rewards are written to a server-side ledger and held. When you cash out, a separate payout worker sends the payment from the distributor wallet and records its transaction.

Live, held and paid: what each word means

Earned / held: recorded in the ledger and waiting for you to cash out. Not on-chain yet.

Paid: sent on-chain from the distributor wallet, with a transaction anyone can check in the XL1 explorer.

Dry run: if the status above ever says dry run, rewards are simulated and nothing is sent.
