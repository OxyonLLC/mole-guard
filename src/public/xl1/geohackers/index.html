GeoHackers

Evidence over assumption.

AlphaA Discord bot on XL1 · made by Oxyon

If something matters, it should be provable: a signed, on-chain record anyone can check without trusting the person making the claim.

GeoHackers verifies wallets, hands out XL1, runs games, and turns what happens into proofs rather than into someone's word for it.

Use it in Discord Getting XL1 On oxyon.io

What the bot does

Six commands. Every one leaves a record.

  • VerifyProve you control a wallet by signing a challenge. Nothing moves, nothing is approved: a signature, not a transaction. How it works
  • Faucet & dripXL1 sent to a wallet you have verified. /faucet for a one-off, /drip on a schedule.
  • Balance & explorer/balance reads your address on-chain; /explorer hands you the link to check it yourself.
  • CowitnessTwo parties co-sign an observation, producing a record neither can alter alone.
  • Mole SpadesThe card game formerly CryptoSpades, now in the XL1 Arcade: finished rounds earn XL1 to your verified wallet. Play
  • Health & docs/health reports what the bot can actually reach. /docs points at the rest.

Getting XL1

It starts in Discord, and that is deliberate.

A faucet you can reach with a URL is a faucet that gets drained by a script, and verifying a wallet does not prevent it: anyone can make a new wallet for nothing, so a signature proves control of a keypair, not that you are a different person from the last request.

A Discord account is not perfect either, but it has history and it has cost. So the bot issues the link and this page only explains it.

  1. JoinThe bot lives in the community Discord.
  2. /verifySign the challenge with your wallet.
  3. /faucetXL1 arrives at the address you proved.
