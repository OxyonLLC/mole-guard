XL1 · Verify

Verify your wallet

Not that you typed the address. That you control it.

In developmentRuns inside GeoHackers

Before GeoHackers sends XL1 anywhere, it needs to know the address belongs to you. You prove that by signing a one-off message with the wallet itself. Signing costs nothing, moves nothing, and approves no transaction.

How it works

Three steps, one signature.

  1. Ask the botRun the verify command in Discord. The bot generates a challenge unique to you that expires, so a signature cannot be replayed or reused by anyone else.
  2. Sign itOpen the link the bot gives you and sign the message it shows. The prompt comes from your wallet extension itself, not from a page asking you to type anything.
  3. Payouts enabledThe bot checks the signature against the challenge and records the address as yours. From then on it sends XL1 there.

The bot does the verifying, not this page. The challenge, the signature check and the record all live in GeoHackers. A second implementation of a signing flow would be a second thing to keep correct, and the wrong place to be clever.

You will never be asked here for a seed phrase, a private key, or a transaction approval. Signing a message is not spending. If anything ever asks you for more than a signature, it is not us.
