WinLEW Website

These are the general terms for the WinLEW website (winlew.co) and its features — verification, the faucet, quiz, games, governance, contributions, and your dashboard. Each Discord bot also has its own terms on the Policies hub.

What WinLEW Website does

  • Verify a wallet and manage Discord Linked Roles
  • Faucet, quiz, and gaming-hub rewards
  • Governance proposals and voting
  • On-chain contributions (Dev / Faucet / X Faucet / Burn)
  • A personal dashboard tying it all together

1 What WinLEW is

WinLEW is a community project around the WinLEW Solana token. The website provides participation tools: wallet verification, a faucet, quizzes, games, governance, contribution tracking, and a personal dashboard. It is community software, provided for the community's use.

2 Accounts & verification

Most features work best once you've verified a wallet you control via a one-time signature (see /verify/). That signature proves ownership and is never a fund-moving transaction. You're responsible for keeping your Discord account and wallet secure.

3 Rewards, faucet, quiz & games

Rewards are community-participation rewards with no guaranteed monetary value. They're discretionary and may change or end. Reward claims are validated server-side; abuse (multi-accounting, botting, forged claims, exploiting bugs) may be reversed and can lead to suspension.

4 Contributions & on-chain actions

When you contribute or transact, you sign and send from your own wallet — the site never takes custody. For contributions, the server builds an exact transfer and independently re-verifies the confirmed transaction on-chain before recording it; it never trusts a typed-in amount. On-chain actions are public and irreversible.

5 Acceptable use

  • One identity per person; no bots or scripts for rewards.
  • Don't attack, overload, or probe the service; don't exploit bugs for gain.
  • Don't impersonate others or post fraudulent content.

6 Token risk & no financial advice

WinLEW is a community token; its value can go to zero. Nothing on the site is financial, investment, tax, or legal advice. Participate only with what you can afford to lose, and do your own research.

7 Availability & limitations

The site is provided "as-is," dependent on Discord, wallet providers, Solana RPC, and hosting. We may change, pause, or restrict features to protect users or keep the project sustainable.

8 Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service evolves. Material changes are reflected here with a new "last updated" date. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.

9 Contact

Questions? Ask in the WinLEW Discord, or see the Policies hub for every WinLEW bot's terms and privacy documents.
