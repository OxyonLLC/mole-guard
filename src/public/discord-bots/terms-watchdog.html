WinLEW Watch Dog

WinLEW Watch Dog is the ecosystem's monitoring bot. It watches WinLEW services and posts status and alerts. It's operational infrastructure — it doesn't verify wallets or handle funds.

What WinLEW Watch Dog does

  • Monitors WinLEW service uptime and response times
  • Posts alerts to Discord when something drifts or fails
  • Feeds the public /health/ status page
  • Never touches wallets, keys, or funds

1 What the service does

The Watch Dog monitors WinLEW's own services and posts alerts to Discord when something goes offline, a metric drifts, or an issue needs attention. You can see the public side of this on the service health page.

2 What it does not do

The Watch Dog does not verify wallets, move tokens, pay rewards, or take custody of anything. It has no access to your wallet or keys. It's a reliability tool for the team and community, not a user-facing wallet service.

3 A note on the status badge

Where the site shows a "service reachable" indicator for the Watch Dog, that reflects whether the underlying service responds — not whether the Discord bot is connected, which this website can't directly observe. We label it honestly rather than guess.

4 Availability & limitations

Provided "as-is." Monitoring itself depends on the same networks and providers it watches, so alerts may lag or miss during an outage. We may change what is monitored or how alerts are delivered at any time.

5 Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service evolves. Material changes are reflected here with a new "last updated" date. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.

6 Contact

Questions? Ask in the WinLEW Discord, or see the Policies hub for every WinLEW bot's terms and privacy documents.
