GeoAgora

This explains what GeoAgora stores to power marketplace browsing, watch alerts, and status checks. It's a distinct ecosystem product and never holds your keys.

📜 Terms 🔒 Privacy
🔒 No custody 🗑️ Delete on request ← All policies

What GeoAgora does

  • Browse marketplace listings from Discord
  • Set and manage watch alerts
  • Check wallet and profile status
  • You keep custody — settlement uses your own keys
✅ Official Discord bot ID 1380425247177707621 Open bot profile ↗

1 What we store

To provide marketplace features, GeoAgora stores the minimum needed:

Discord user ID
Identifies your account for alerts and preferences.
Linked wallet / profile
Public address(es) or profile identifiers you connect — never keys.
Watch alerts & preferences
The listings or conditions you asked to be notified about.

2 What we never have

Never your private keys, seed phrase, or spending authority. Any purchase settles with keys you control on the relevant system.

3 How it is used

  • To show listings and check wallet/profile status you request.
  • To deliver the watch alerts you set up.

Not used for advertising; never sold.

4 Who it is shared with

  • Discord — to deliver messages and alerts.
  • Public blockchain / marketplace systems — status and settlement data on those systems is inherently public.

5 Retention & your rights

Preferences and records are kept while your account uses the service. Request access or deletion in the WinLEW Discord; settled on-chain records are public and permanent.

6 Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service evolves. Material changes are reflected here with a new "last updated" date. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.

7 Contact

Questions? Ask in the WinLEW Discord, or see the Policies hub for every WinLEW bot's terms and privacy documents.
