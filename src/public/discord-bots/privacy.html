WinLEW Website

This explains what winlew.co stores as you use its features, why, how long, and how to have it removed. The site never holds your private keys.

📜 Terms 🔒 Privacy
🔒 No custody 🗑️ Delete on request ← All policies

What WinLEW Website does

  • Verify a wallet and manage Discord Linked Roles
  • Faucet, quiz, and gaming-hub rewards
  • Governance proposals and voting
  • On-chain contributions (Dev / Faucet / X Faucet / Burn)
  • A personal dashboard tying it all together

1 What we store

Depending on which features you use, the website may store:

Discord user ID
Your account identifier across features.
Linked wallet address(es)
Public Solana addresses you verify — never private keys.
Verification records
When you verified and when the cycle expires; one-time challenges (expire fast).
Faucet / quiz / game history
Claims, points, streaks, and validated reward records.
Contribution records
On-chain-verified contributions you make, for the Clout leaderboard.
Profile preferences
Optional display name, bio, and avatar you set for your public profile.
Session tokens
Short-lived tokens so a signed-in session works; they expire on their own.

2 What we never have

We never receive or store your private keys, seed phrase, or spending authority. Signatures prove ownership only. There is no custody.

3 How it is used

  • To verify you and keep roles and eligibility accurate.
  • To run rewards, leaderboards, governance, and your dashboard.
  • To validate contributions and reward claims and prevent abuse.

Not used for advertising; never sold.

4 Who it is shared with

  • Discord — for login, roles, and messages.
  • Public blockchain — balances, payouts, and contributions are public on Solana.
  • Infrastructure providers — hosting, database, and RPC providers process data to run the service; they aren't given your data to use for their own purposes.

5 Retention

Records are kept while they're needed for your account, accurate leaderboards, and payout accounting. You can request deletion of your off-chain data; transactions already confirmed on Solana are public and permanent.

6 Your rights & deleting your data

To see or delete your stored data, email support@winlew.co from any address and include your Discord username (or ask in the WinLEW Discord). You don't need to install anything or sign in to ask. We confirm and complete deletion within 30 days. Deletion removes your off-chain records (account links, verification records, reward and game history, profile); it can't alter on-chain history, and records we must keep for payout accounting are kept only as long as needed.

7 The WinLEW Android app

The WinLEW app (co.winlew.app) opens winlew.co inside the app, so everything above applies to it the same way. The app itself adds no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. Its Android permissions are: internet access (to load winlew.co), notifications (optional reminders you allow, shown by the phone itself; we don't send push messages), and restart/wake permissions so those reminders survive a phone restart. Wallet actions open your own wallet app; WinLEW never sees your keys. The app is for adults (18+).

8 Cookies & tracking

The site uses only what's needed to keep you signed in and remember basic preferences (e.g. a session token and local settings). It does not run third-party advertising trackers.

9 Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service evolves. Material changes are reflected here with a new "last updated" date. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.

10 Contact

Questions? Email support@winlew.co, ask in the WinLEW Discord, or see the Policies hub for every WinLEW bot's terms and privacy documents.
