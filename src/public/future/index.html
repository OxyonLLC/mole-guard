The problem

Most of what we rely on is a claim, not evidence.

We trust these every day because of who presents them, not because we can check them.

The Oxyon model

From observation to better decisions.

Each step makes the next one checkable. Trust is the output of the chain, never the input.

  1. Observation

    Something is seen, measured or done.

  2. Evidence

    It is recorded, hashed and signed at its origin.

  3. Verification

    Anyone can recheck the signature, hash and sources.

  4. Confidence

    Evidence is weighed, with the reasoning shown.

  5. Trust

    Earned from proof, not assumed from authority.

  6. Better Decisions

    Made on what can be shown, not on what is said.

Runs entirely in your browser with the Web Crypto API. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Where XL1 fits

Oxyon is not another blockchain. A blockchain is one optional step: it gives evidence a public, timestamped receipt that nobody can quietly rewrite.

  1. Observationsomething happens
  2. Evidencehashed + signed
  3. GeoProofstandard event
  4. Verificationreceipt with reasons
  5. XL1 anchoroptional receipt
  6. Independent checkby anyone

On XL1 we use anchoring (batch roots of proofs), receipts and provenance (who signed what, when). Proof events are built on XYO payloads and bound witnesses; GeoProof defines the schema and does not reinvent the cryptography.
