HODL Rewards

Hold, take part, get rewarded.

The faucet rewards showing up. The quiz rewards knowing things. Games reward playing. HODL Rewards recognise staying, and staying involved.

While an epoch is running, distributions are scheduled for each Tuesday. Each verified holder's reward is a small percentage of their balance: 0.10% as a base, rising to 0.15% for taking part on all seven days of the week before. Larger balances earn more, up to clear caps, and every payment comes from a fixed weekly community allocation.

🗓️ Scheduled every Tuesday

Not to be confused with the HODL + Liquidity Campaign — a separate, time-limited draw for 1,000,000 $WinLEW that closed on 1 October 2026. HODL Rewards below are the ongoing weekly programme.

What this is, plainly. A weekly reward worked out as a percentage of the WinLEW you hold, paid from a capped community allocation. It is not staking: you deposit nothing, nothing is locked, and your tokens never leave your wallet. It is not guaranteed: the rates apply only while an epoch is running, Tuesday is the scheduled day rather than a promise (a distribution can be delayed, paused or skipped), a busy week can be scaled down to fit the pool, and an epoch can be ended. A reward paid in WinLEW is only worth what WinLEW is worth.

Limits governance cannot raise

Governance chooses each epoch's parameters, but only within these. They are not themselves votable, and an epoch that breaches one cannot be activated at all.

    The caps exist so that the largest wallets cannot take the whole allocation: only part of a large balance counts, and nobody receives more than the per-person cap in a week.

    How to qualify

    • A verified wallet, linked to your Discord account and not expired.
    • Holding at least the epoch's minimum — a small amount — when the snapshot is taken.
    • Not banned, and not linked to a banned wallet.

    Qualification is per account, not per wallet. The verified wallets on one account are added together and treated as one holder with one payment, so splitting tokens across wallets changes nothing.

    How your reward is worked out

    1. Your counted balance. The WinLEW in your verified wallets at the Tuesday snapshot, counting at most 5,000,000 WinLEW.
    2. Your active days. How many of the 7 full UTC days before the snapshot day you took part on (normally Tuesday to Monday). The snapshot day itself does not count.
    3. Your rate. Counted balance × the rate for your active days (table below).
    4. The caps. No more than 2,500 WinLEW to any one person in a week. If everyone's rewards together would exceed the weekly pool, every payment is reduced by the same percentage so the total fits. Amounts are rounded down to whole tokens.

    What counts as an active day

    Any one of these, on a UTC calendar day, from your verified account:

    • a confirmed faucet claim
    • a validated game reward claim
    • answering the web quiz
    • /mole checkin in the WinLEW Discord

    Several activities on the same day count as one day. Missing a day only costs that day, never the days before it. Each week is counted fresh, and only from server-recorded times. Proof of Value scores do not add a bonus on top of these rates.

    Decided every three months

    Each epoch runs about three months (13 weeks), and its terms — the weekly allocation, the minimum holding, the rate table — are fixed for its duration and published here. Near the end of an epoch the community votes on the next one. Past epochs are never rewritten; what a week paid stays on record.

    Governance chooses within hard limits set in code (listed above). No vote can raise the weekly rate ceiling, the counted-balance cap or the per-person weekly cap.

    Not verified yet?

    One signed message. No transaction, no fees, no seed phrase. You qualify from the next snapshot.
