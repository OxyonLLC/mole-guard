Your wallet address is your inbox.

No phone number. No email address. Messages travel on XL1 as part of a signed transaction you can verify on the explorer.

Message + value, one transaction

Send a message to any XL1 address and optionally attach XL1 to it. The transfer and the message are signed together in a single transaction.

Private messages

End-to-end encrypted: only the recipient's wallet can read them. As with any transaction, who sent it, to whom, when, and any XL1 attached stay visible on-chain.

Spam protection

Messages from first-time senders wait quietly under Requests — no badge, no notification — until you accept them. Block anyone, anytime.

Messaging is in beta. Public messages are permanent and readable by anyone on XL1 — never send secrets or recovery phrases.

What Oxyon Wallet does

Multi-chain asset management

Manage Ethereum, Solana, XL1, and XYO-related assets from a single wallet experience.

Tokens & NFTs

View supported coins, tokens, NFT collections, metadata, and ownership status as the wallet expands.

dApp connections

Connect to supported decentralized applications and review every request before approving it.

Local-first privacy

Your recovery phrase and private keys are encrypted locally. Oxyon Wallet does not run user accounts or analytics.

Safer signing

Transactions are decoded and risk-checked before you sign, with clear warnings when something can't be verified.

Quantum-ready identity

A post-quantum (ML-DSA-65) identity derived from your seed, with a signed attestation binding it to your XL1 address.

Part of Oxyon.

Oxyon builds decision infrastructure — turning observations into evidence anyone can verify. Oxyon Wallet is where you hold assets, sign, and send verifiable messages, and the access point for GeoAgora, GeoJustice, and GeoProof.
