03 / THE PART WE COULD HAVE DELETED

It started on Base.
We're telling you because we could have just not.

“Wrong chain,
right joke.”

— the whole correction, honestly

The original was minted on Base on Nov 1, 2025, at 12:40 AM. Through Zora, because it was there and so was the drink. One billion supply, a platypus, and no plan whatsoever.

It's moving to Solana for the boring, correct reason: that's where everything else already is. The Discord, the bots, the wallets people already verified, $WinLEW itself — all Solana. A meme that lives one chain away from its own community isn't a meme, it's a chore.

Nobody got hurt in the detour. There were zero outside buyers — the only holder was the guy who made it. No one to make whole, nothing to migrate, no bridge required. That's the entire reason this page can be relaxed about it.

The old Base contract still exists — contracts are forever — and its liquidity is being withdrawn so it can't be traded. It's linked above so you can check it, not so you can buy it. Don't buy it. It is not this token, it is not $WinLEW, and it is not coming back.

We don't hide. Ask anything.
