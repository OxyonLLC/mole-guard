Delete your WinLEW account and data

This page is for the WinLEW website (winlew.co) and the WinLEW Android app (co.winlew.app), published by Oxyon LLC. You can ask for deletion of your whole account or just some of your data.

How to ask

  1. Email support@winlew.co with the subject Delete my WinLEW data.
  2. Include your Discord username and/or the wallet address you linked, so we can find your records.
  3. Say whether you want everything deleted, or only part of it (for example your profile or a linked wallet).
Email a deletion request

You can also ask in the WinLEW Discord. You don't need to sign in or install anything to ask. We confirm by reply and finish within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account link (Discord ID) and linked wallet addresses
  • Verification records and sign-in sessions
  • Quiz, game, faucet and reward history; leaderboard entries
  • Your profile (display name, bio, avatar)

What is kept, and for how long

  • Blockchain transactions (payouts, transfers) are public on Solana / XYO Layer One and can't be changed or deleted by anyone.
  • Payout accounting records (wallet address, amount, date) are kept only as long as they are needed for payout accounting, then deleted.
  • Abuse-prevention records (a ban on a wallet or account) are kept while the ban applies.
