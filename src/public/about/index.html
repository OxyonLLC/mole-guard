WinLEW & Oxyon

The technology has its own home now

WinLEW is the community, token and participation ecosystem. The tools we built along the way (the wallet, verification and proof systems, our XL1 infrastructure) are now developed under Oxyon ↗, built by the same team. WinLEW is not being replaced or renamed; it is where much of that technology is used first. GeoWallet is now Oxyon Wallet: same wallet, same keys.
