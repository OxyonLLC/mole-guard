Module details

Signals, not wallet size

WinLEW shifts incentives: what you do matters more than what you hold. Signals are designed to value effort and correctness.

Guardrails first

Before scaling rewards, the system gets hardened. Rate limits, consistency checks, and abuse detection keep the economy healthy.

Community tuning loop

Rules can be tuned as behavior is observed. The point is fairness + sustainability, not “set it and forget it.”
