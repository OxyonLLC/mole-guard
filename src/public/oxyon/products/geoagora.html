GeoAgora

Verify marketplaces.

Early design · Prototyping

GeoAgora is the trusted-exchange module: a marketplace where the things that decide a trade — who the seller is, what the item is, what the history shows — are proofs, not promises.

The design

Reputation you can recompute.

  1. IdentitySellers and buyers are verified identities, not usernames.
  2. ListingA listing carries evidence of what it claims to be.
  3. ExchangeThe trade itself produces a verifiable record for both parties.
  4. ReputationHistory accumulates as receipts anyone can check — not editable star ratings.
  5. DiscoverySearch and ranking weighted by verified history, transparently.

Why it needs the platform

A marketplace is where trust gets expensive.

Fake sellers, forged listings, and farmed ratings all exploit the same gap: marketplaces assert trust they cannot show. GeoAgora is designed on top of the other modules — identities from Oxyon Wallet, verdicts from GeoJustice, presence from GeoPresence, proofs in the GeoProof format — so the marketplace never has to be believed on its own authority.

Status

Where it stands — honestly.

GeoAgora is the earliest module on the platform: design work and wallet connectivity groundwork exist; the marketplace itself is not built yet. It ships after the modules it depends on are proven — that order is deliberate.
