GeoPresence

Verify location.

In development · Working check-in layer, v1.1.x

GeoPresence turns “I was there” into evidence. A check-in layer that binds an identity, a place, and a moment into a proof — with the anti-spoofing and privacy work done before anything is published.

How a check-in becomes proof

Challenge, capture, seal.

  1. ChallengeThe identity signs a one-use challenge — fresh, expiring, and never replayable.
  2. CaptureLocation is captured with its accuracy measured, not assumed.
  3. ValidateFreshness, accuracy bounds, and cooldowns are enforced before acceptance.
  4. SplitRaw coordinates stay private; the public proof carries only a location hash.
  5. PublishThe sealed proof joins the participant’s verifiable history.

Capabilities

What’s built.

  • Signed challengesWallet-based identity across ecosystems, proven by signature — one use, then gone.
  • Accuracy validationA reading that can’t meet the accuracy bar doesn’t become a proof.
  • Replay resistanceFreshness windows and per-identity cooldowns close the obvious cheats.
  • Privacy splitPrivate raw storage, public hash-only proofs. Presence without surveillance.
  • Participation historyStreaks, awards, and leaderboard-ready stats derived from verified check-ins.
  • Durable storageDatabase-backed with graceful fallback — a check-in is never silently lost.

Status

What exists today.

A working starter with the complete check-in flow: challenge issuance and validation, browser location capture, the privacy split, and participation stats. Reward adapters are stubbed and honest about it. Proof formats align with the GeoProof presence schema; anchoring rides the same path as the rest of the platform.
