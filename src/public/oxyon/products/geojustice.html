GeoJustice

Verify actions.

Active · Self-certifying registry with CI

GeoJustice is the verification engine: an evidence-first, append-only, replayable registry that discovers, verifies, scores, fingerprints, and explains digital assets and the actions around them — so “is this legitimate?” gets an answer backed by preserved evidence, not opinion.

How it works

Every claim traces to an artifact.

  1. DiscoverContracts and collections are found across networks, exchanges, explorers, and public code.
  2. PreserveEvery source document is stored as a content-addressed evidence artifact.
  3. VerifyClaims are checked against the preserved evidence — exact and authoritative only.
  4. ScoreTrust scores and fingerprints are derived deterministically from the record.
  5. ExplainEvery verdict can be traced back to the artifacts that produced it.
  6. ReplayThe whole registry re-derives identically from its evidence — and proves it.

Capabilities

What ships in the registry.

  • Read-only APIQuery intelligence without the ability to rewrite history.
  • Typed SDKProgrammatic access with the same guarantees as the API.
  • Investigation dashboardTrace any verdict through its evidence chain, visually.
  • Self-certificationA single command proves the corpus is consistent and reproducible.
  • Trust snapshotsVersioned trust publications with explicit policy semantics.
  • Release anchoringRegistry releases are themselves anchored and attestable on the network.

Design guardrails

Exact evidence only — by design.

No fuzzy or probabilistic matching. No ownership surveillance. No marketplace logic. The registry answers only what the preserved evidence supports, deterministically. If the evidence isn’t there, neither is the claim.
