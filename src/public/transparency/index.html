Graduation

🎓 Graduation Milestone

Confirmed milestone date: July 22, 2026.

That date is based on the creation time of the verified PumpSwap market for the permanent WinLEW mint, now surfaced by pump.fun, DexScreener, and GeckoTerminal.

What changed: WinLEW moved beyond its original pump.fun launch phase into a post-graduation market structure with a verified PumpSwap market and continued Raydium availability.

📈 Tokenomics

The tokenomics figures communicated at launch represent the original allocation and are preserved here as historical documentation, not edited retroactively. Since then, allocations have moved as described above: a 50M lock, a 15M team lock, a 25M faucet-runway lock, a 19.19M Developer Reserve self-lock (added Aug 11 2026 — 3-year cliff, then 2M/year through 2033) and two 20M Burn Locks (added Aug 16 2026 — 40M moved out of the burn wallet so burn-staged supply cannot be spent before its date; one unlocks Aug 1 2027, the other in three annual instalments 2029–2031) were placed via Jupiter Lock, and two separate Streamflow vesting contracts hold a further 22M+ and 7M+ — with dedicated reserve wallets funding the faucet (both Discord and website) and ongoing development. Cross-reference any historical figure against the live components above — that's the actual current state, not the original plan.

✅ What is not excluded, and why

A project can shrink its reported circulating supply by calling any wallet a reserve. These balances are all project-accessible or freely tradeable, so they stay counted as circulating.

Staged for burn is not burned. The burn wallet's balance is counted as circulating on this page. Those tokens still exist and the mint still counts them; they leave supply only when a Solana burn instruction destroys them. Verified burns that have actually executed are on the Burn Center.
