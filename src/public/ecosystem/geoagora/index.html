GeoAgora
A real marketplace with one identity across chains.

GeoAgora is a multi-chain marketplace where your Ethereum, Base, Polygon, and Solana wallets — plus ENS and Unstoppable Domains names — all roll up into a single, portable identity anchored on XL1, instead of a different disconnected profile per chain.

🖼️ Multi-chain marketplace 🪪 One identity, every chain Coming Soon
Status
GeoAgora is real and already in beta development — it's just not ready to be exposed publicly yet. This page describes the vision, not a live product you can open today.
What is it

The wallet comes first — but you are the identity

Most marketplaces treat each connected wallet as its own separate account. GeoAgora flips that: it treats your XL1 identity as the anchor, and lets Ethereum, Base, Polygon, and Solana wallets — along with ENS and Unstoppable Domains names — all link to that one identity. Trade across chains, and your reputation, listings, and history stay attached to you, not to whichever wallet happened to sign that day.

"A real multi-chain marketplace with XL1 identity at the core."

What it's for

More than just buying and selling

🖼️ Marketplace

Listings, offers, and bundles across the chains it supports, with a shared price/event history.

🪪 Portable identity

One public profile that follows you across every linked wallet and chain — proof and reputation you can show anywhere.

🧬 Protocol Studio

A tool for authoring and inspecting on-chain data schemas — for people building on top of the ecosystem, not just trading.
