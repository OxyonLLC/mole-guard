What is it

A wallet built around one identity, not six separate apps

Oxyon Wallet (formerly GeoWallet) is a non-custodial, multi-chain wallet: your keys stay with you, and one wallet covers Ethereum, Polygon, Optimism, Base, Solana, and XL1 (the XYO Layer One chain) instead of juggling a different app per chain. It's built for the XYO ecosystem first, but works as a general-purpose EVM + Solana wallet for anyone.

The exact same app ships three ways — as a browser-based PWA, a Chrome extension with a MetaMask-compatible provider, and a native iOS/Android app via Capacitor (in development) — so you pick whichever fits how you actually use a wallet.

The reason to trust it

GeoShield: it would rather say "I don't know" than say "safe" wrongly

GeoShield is Oxyon Wallet's built-in transaction-safety engine. Before you sign anything — a swap, a token approval, a signature request — it decodes exactly what that transaction actually does, on-device, and shows you a plain verdict:

🔴 Red

A known-dangerous pattern — like an unlimited token approval — decoded and flagged clearly.

🟠 Orange

"Cannot verify." GeoShield's own rule is to confess when it doesn't recognize something, never guess that it's safe.

🟡 Yellow

Understood, but worth a second look before signing.

🟢 Green

Fully decoded and recognized as an expected, low-risk action.

"Decode-or-confess" is architectural law inside Oxyon Wallet: unknown calldata is never shown as safe just because it's unfamiliar — the exact opposite of how most wallet-drainer scams get people to sign away their tokens.

Under the hood

Built as one monorepo, not three separate projects

A TypeScript/React/Vite web app, a Chrome MV3 extension, and a Capacitor-wrapped mobile app all share the same core packages — including the vault (AES-GCM encryption with a very high PBKDF2 iteration count) and an "Exposure Ledger" that finds your active token approvals across chains and gives you the exact calldata to revoke them.

Oxyon Wallet & WinLEW

Built by the same people, not the same product

Oxyon Wallet isn't a WinLEW feature — it's an Oxyon product, built by the team behind WinLEW. It launched as GeoWallet and took the Oxyon name when Oxyon became the home for the technology we build; your recovery phrase and addresses did not change. WinLEW links to it here because it's a genuinely useful wallet for anyone holding $WinLEW or interacting with the XYO/XL1 ecosystem, not because WinLEW built it.
