GeoJustice
Exact evidence, not a guess dressed up as a score.

GeoJustice discovers, verifies, and scores XYO/XL1 smart contracts and NFT collections across chains, exchanges, and code repositories — and for every claim it makes, it publishes the exact evidence behind it. Deliberately not a wallet, a marketplace, or an AI best-guess tool.

Status
GeoJustice is real and already working behind the scenes — it's just not ready to be exposed publicly yet.
What is it

Trust you can retrace, not just take on faith

GeoJustice is being built as the canonical source of explainable trust decisions for contracts, collections, operators, and releases in the XYO/XL1 ecosystem. Every verdict it produces traces back to a preserved piece of evidence — never a fuzzy AI guess and never a self-reported claim taken at face value.

"GeoJustice becomes the canonical source of explainable trust decisions for assets, contracts, collections, operators, and releases."

What it's for

The system that produces the trust other tools consume

📋 Human + machine dossiers

A readable explanation and a machine-checkable decision for every contract or collection it evaluates.

🔁 Deterministic under replay

Run the same evidence through it twice, get the same answer twice — trust that doesn't drift.

⛓️ Release anchoring

Attests release artifacts on-chain, so "this is the exact build that shipped" is provable, not just claimed.

"If GeoWallet is a consumer of trust, GeoJustice should be the system that produces, explains, and publishes that trust."

GeoJustice & WinLEW

Same builder, separate project

GeoJustice is a sister project from the same team behind WinLEW, not a WinLEW feature — its focus is XYO/XL1 contract and collection trust more broadly, with no confirmed WinLEW-specific integration today.

It is an Oxyon project, built by the team behind WinLEW. Its current status is on oxyon.io ↗.

Nothing on this page is a promise of a launch date. Check back, or watch WinLEW's own announcement channels for when GeoJustice actually opens up.
