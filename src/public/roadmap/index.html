Upcoming

Direction we've committed to — quarters, not fake dates. Planned means planned, not guaranteed.

🤝 Partnership Q3 2026

We plan to welcome @2brz from X into the WinLEW ecosystem. This collaboration is intended to strengthen community engagement and expand educational content.

🗓 Planned Q3 / Q4 2026

More Educational Content

Continue expanding quizzes, improving documentation, and improving guides for new users.

Open Quiz →Open Whitepaper →
🗓 Planned Q4 2026

XL1 Integration

One of our largest engineering priorities, and not yet started. Today the only XL1 touchpoint is read-only — we can read a linked XL1 wallet to build a profile link. The intent is to begin recording WinLEW data to XL1 where it genuinely adds verifiability, once a public mechanism for doing so is available.

⏸ Deferred

Cross-Chain Bridge

Deferred, not removed — created with future interoperability with XL1 in mind. No official public XL1 bridging mechanism exists yet, so implementation has not started.

🗓 Planned

Platform v2 Enhancements

Advanced analytics and multi-language support — not started. No repository evidence of implementation.

🤝 Partnership Q4 2026

Sponsorship Announcement

During December we plan to announce an organization or entity that WinLEW will sponsor to help grow their ecosystem. We are intentionally keeping details private until discussions are complete — we have already had the opportunity to test one of their projects and look forward to sharing more when appropriate.
