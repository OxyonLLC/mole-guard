WinLEW doesn’t think recognition should come from saying what you own or what you’ve done. We verify it. Linking your wallet lets WinLEW recognise your holdings, participation and eligibility across Discord and the rest of the ecosystem — so the evidence speaks instead of you having to.

🔐 We never need your private key

💰 There are no verification fees

You prove ownership by signing a one-time message with your wallet. A signature is not a transaction — it moves nothing and approves no spending.

How to verify

  1. Log in with Discord (official discord.com OAuth — we never see your password).
  2. Connect your wallet and sign the one-time verification message.
  3. Your roles sync in the WinLEW Discord, and your dashboard unlocks.

What verification unlocks

Verification publishes five values about your wallet to Discord, and nothing beyond them: that it is verified, whether it holds any WinLEW, whether it crosses the 1M and 10M marks, and your whole-number WinLEW balance (which is what lets Discord build threshold roles). Discord decides which roles those values qualify you for — WinLEW never sees or stores your Discord password. Your balance is public on the blockchain either way; this simply connects it to your account so a role can be issued from it.

🗓️ HODL Rewards

Verifying does more than unlock a role. A verified wallet also qualifies you for HODL Rewards — paid from a fixed weekly community allocation, with distributions scheduled for each Tuesday while an epoch is running.

It is not staking: you deposit nothing, nothing is locked, and your tokens never leave your wallet. Each reward is 0.10% to 0.15% of your verified balance, with the higher rates for taking part on more days of the week before. Only part of a very large balance counts, each person's weekly reward is capped, and a busy week is scaled down to fit the allocation. Rewards are not guaranteed.

🏛️ Governance

The same verification decides whether you can vote. You do not need a separate governance wallet — one verified wallet is one identity across the whole ecosystem.

A verified wallet holding WinLEW gets one free vote. Verifying is what earns your voice; buying extra vote weight on top is a separate, capped thing with its own much higher bar. The exact thresholds and the honest limits of what a vote can guarantee are set out on the governance page.
