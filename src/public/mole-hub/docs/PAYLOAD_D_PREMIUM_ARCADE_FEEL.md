# Payload D — Premium Arcade Feel

## Goal
Make Burrow Blaster feel more polished, responsive, and arcade-like without changing the already-working fair difficulty loop.

## Included
- Muzzle flashes for default, spread, and laser fire.
- Stronger enemy death bursts and expanding shockwave rings.
- Pickup burst polish for shards, risk shards, spread, laser, shield, and burst cores.
- Shield absorb FX with a visible cyan ring and stronger feedback text.
- Exhaust trail behind Lew's drill-ship.
- Arcade wave/round banners.
- More compact premium HUD panel for lives, weapon state, rank, combo, and near misses.

## Notes
Payload D builds on Payload C and preserves the round loop, rewards, hazard safety rules, and enemy variety pass.
